About the studio

BLOCKBROS makes games from Hawaii and ships them where people can watch the work happen.

The story

BLOCKBROS is a game studio in Hawaii. The cabinet is neon and competitive, with island heat instead of a corporate lobby. The studio ships games people can play today.

International Robot League is the flagship, live now as 3D robot fighting with online multiplayer. Super Fly Ninja, The Great Wagon Trail, and Mugshot Mayhem are live on gd.games. Quick Fun is one door for the short chaotic hits. Star Catch is the TRON game. The Daily Puzzle is free, with optional Nile testnet extras and no wallet required to play.

This site is the studio home at blockbros808.com. The short share page for socials and play links stays at /links/.
