Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026 Last updated: October 31, 2026

Aloha! This page explains what information Block Bros collects when you play our games or visit our website, what we do with it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain English so players and parents can actually read it.

If you have questions, email us at blockbros1808@gmail.com.

1. Who we are

"Block Bros," "BLOCKBROS," "we," "us," and "our" mean Block Bros, an independent studio in Hawaiʻi, USA.

This policy covers:

  • Our website, blockbros808.com (including blockbros808.github.io)
  • Our games, including International Robot League, The Great Wagon Trail, Super Fly Ninja, Mugshot Mayhem, the Quick Fun pack, Star Catch, and Daily Puzzle
  • Our Block Bros play hub app for Android (package com.blockbros808.play), when offered — same Soft 13+, free/no paywall rules as the website play hub at play.blockbros808.com
  • Our pages on itch.io, gd.games, and other places where we publish our games (together, the "Services")

Some of our games run on other companies' platforms, such as itch.io, gd.games/GDevelop, and GitHub Pages. Those platforms have their own privacy policies and may collect information we never see. See Section 5.

2. What we collect

We try to collect as little as possible. Here's what we may collect, depending on the game and how you play.

Things you give us

  • Display names and robot names. In games like International Robot League, you can name your player or robot. These names may appear to other players and on leaderboards. Please don't use your real name, age, school, or anything personal.
  • Messages to us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.
  • Block Bros account (free signup; soft 13+). You can browse the hub (shelves, Square list, rules, News teasers) without an account. Soft 13+ — you must be 13 or older to create an account. Don’t sign up if you’re under 13. Free signup uses a magic link emailed to you (no password stored).
  • When we ask for an account. Browsing the hub, playing games, and Ask AI / prompt-to-make do not need an account. A free Block Bros account is needed only when you want to put a game on the platform — on the public Square or a shelf — whether you made it with Ask AI or uploaded a real game, and to load a game to your phone (when offered). That way we have a contact email if something goes wrong. Everything is free — no paywall.
  • Account details we store. Email address; nickname / display name; settings you choose; games you made or posted; and (when signed in) site history such as games you played or ♥ liked on the hub. We do not store a password for magic-link login. Your email is never shown on Square or publicly — only your nickname is. That same rule applies in BlockChat.
  • How we use account email. To send magic-link sign-in, run your login, and tell you about your games (review pass, fail, or what to fix — on-screen and by email). No ads or newsletters from us by default, and we never sell your email. Optional Blockbros News is separate and only via double opt-in (see Section 6).
  • Optional signup perk. If we offer a signup perk or bonus, it is kid-safe and has no real-money value. We do not sell in-app purchases or cash prizes unless we update this policy and Tony / Block Bros says money features are live.
  • Quick-chat selections. Where a game offers chat, we aim to use preset phrases ("Good game!", "Nice shot!") instead of free typing. We may log which preset was sent so we can keep games fair and safe.
  • BlockChat messages. BlockChat on play.blockbros808.com is optional free-typing chat for ages 13+. Guests may read without an account. To post, you must sign in with a free Block Bros magic-link account (Soft 13+). When you post, we store the message text, channel or room, time, and your account nickname. Your nickname is set from your Block Bros account (not typed freely in chat) and is public in chat; your email is never shown in chat or on Square. Posts in #showcase may be held for moderator review before they are public. We use this to run the chat, moderate, enforce Soft 13+ and house rules, and keep the community safe — not to build advertising profiles. Don’t use BlockChat if you’re under 13.

Things collected when you play

  • Guest browse and play. You can browse the hub, play games, and try Ask AI / prompt without a Block Bros account. Optional guest likes may still use the salted lock described below.
  • Play a saved game. Our “play a saved game” / load-to-phone opener runs on your device. The file stays on your phone or computer; we don’t receive or upload that file. Soft 13+ still applies. BlockChat is not offered as a downloadable pack.
  • Block Bros sign-in. Free Block Bros account (magic link) stores the account details above. Keep access to your email safe — you’re responsible for activity under your account.
  • Other platform sign-in. Some games also let you sign in through a host platform (for example GDevelop/gd.games or itch.io). Those accounts follow that platform’s privacy policy. We may receive a user ID and username from that provider. We never see that platform’s password.
  • Gameplay and leaderboard data. Scores, match results, wins and losses, and progress. Leaderboard entries (name + score) are public.
  • Device and technical data. Things like browser type, device type, operating system, language, screen size, crash or error reports, and timestamps. This helps the games run and helps us fix bugs.
  • Approximate region (if used). For online matches we may use a rough location (like country or region, based on IP address) to connect you to nearby players or servers. We don't collect precise GPS location.
  • Hub safety signals (staff only). To keep the hub fair and working, we may keep short-lived operations signals: for example BlockChat presence (display nick, room, last seen), coarse page traffic counts (path and hour), and abuse signals (reports, hides, rate-limit blocks). Unused decoy paths (fake admin or login URLs that real players never need) may briefly log path, time, browser type, and a hashed IP only — never account emails, nicknames, cookies, tokens, or account IDs, and never a copy of player sessions, chats, Ask AI, or Square. We do not build personal profiles for advertising. IP addresses, if used at all for abuse control, are hashed and not shown as a personal file. These signals are for staff tools only, kept for a limited time (about 30 days unless law or an active safety case needs longer), then dropped or turned into anonymous totals.
  • Hub visit counts (anonymous). On play.blockbros808.com we show a public “visitors today” total. We store counts only (page views, unique visits per Hawaiʻi day, and path totals) — no names, IPs, user-agents, fingerprints, or advertising profiles. Your browser may keep a same-day local-storage flag so we don’t count you twice that day. Rate limits may hold an IP in memory briefly and never write it to disk.
  • Hub online count (anonymous). On the play hub we may also show how many people are online now as a number only. That total comes from short-lived BlockChat presence heartbeats (about a minute). The public count does not show names, IPs, rooms, or who is who.

  • Hub game likes (optional, anonymous). On the play hub you may tap ♥ to like a game. Likes are optional. Public totals are counts only (per game) — no names, accounts required, raw device IDs, IPs on disk, or advertising profiles. Guests can like. Soft 13+ still applies. To keep likes fair (about one lasting like per game per device, with same-day undo), we may keep a salted one-way lock token derived from the game and your device — not a readable device ID and not your IP. Rate limits may also hold an IP in memory briefly. Your browser may keep a local flag so the heart stays filled for you.

  • Cookies and similar storage. Our games and site may use browser storage (cookies, local storage) to save settings, progress, and (on the hub) a same-day visitor flag for the public door counter, a local like flag for ♥ buttons, and (if you sign in) an account session so you stay logged in. We do not use advertising cookies. Hosting platforms (GitHub Pages, gd.games, itch.io) may still log technical data under their own policies.

Crypto testnet features (Star Catch and Daily Puzzle extras)

  • If you choose to connect a TronLink wallet on the TRON Nile testnet, we receive your public wallet address and any testnet transactions you approve (like claiming a BlockBros Badge).
  • Wallet addresses and blockchain transactions are public by design and stored on the blockchain. We cannot delete or change them.
  • We never ask for or receive your private key or seed phrase. Never share those with anyone, including us.
  • These features are optional and testnet only. They have no real money value.

Things we do NOT collect on purpose

We don't ask for your real name, home address, phone number, photos, voice, precise location, or payment card details. Purchases are handled by the platform (see Section 5).

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Run the games: matchmaking, saving progress, showing leaderboards
  • Keep play fair and safe: spotting cheating, removing offensive names, enforcing our Terms
  • Fix bugs and improve our games
  • Show anonymous public hub visit totals, online counts, and optional game like totals on play.blockbros808.com
  • Reply when you contact us
  • Run Block Bros accounts (magic-link login, settings, games you made, signed-in play/like history, load-to-phone when offered)
  • Tell account holders about their game’s Square review (pass, fail, or what to fix)
  • Send optional Blockbros News edition emails and studio news only after double opt-in (Section 6)
  • Deliver testnet features you choose to use, like badge claims
  • Follow the law and protect our rights and our players

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles about you.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising (as California law uses that term).

We share information only:

  • With platforms and service providers that host or run our games and site or send our account and review emails (for example itch.io, GDevelop/gd.games, GitHub Pages, our email-delivery provider, and any game-server or leaderboard service we use), and only as needed to provide the Services
  • Publicly, by design: leaderboard names and scores, display/robot names other players can see, anonymous hub visitor/view totals, online counts, and game like totals on play.blockbros808.com, and testnet wallet activity on the blockchain
  • For legal reasons: if the law requires it, or to protect someone's safety, or our rights
  • If Block Bros changes hands: if we merge with or are acquired by another company, information may transfer, and this policy (or one at least as protective) will still apply

5. Third-party platforms, ads, and payments

Some of our games are hosted on other companies' platforms. When you play there, their privacy policies apply to what they collect:

  • gd.games / GDevelop. May show ads or offer "earn coins"-style rewards and may handle sign-in and leaderboards. Ads may be served by GDevelop's ad partners. See GDevelop's privacy policy.
  • itch.io. Handles downloads, accounts, and payments. See itch.io's privacy policy.
  • GitHub Pages. Hosts our website. GitHub may log technical data like IP addresses. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • TronLink and the TRON network. Wallet software and blockchain are run by others. Block Bros is not affiliated with TRON, Tron Inc., or TRON DAO.
  • Payments. If we offer a paid version (for example, a one-time ~$1 purchase), payment is processed by the store or platform. We don't receive your full card details.

We don't control these third parties. Please review their policies.

6. Blockbros News email (optional)

We may offer an optional Blockbros News email: a marketing newsletter with our daily/weekly game paper (world games news, Block Bros wire, Island Devs, Made in 808, events, comics, and related studio updates).

  • How you join. Only if you submit the Newsletter subscribe form on our site (e.g. /news/) and then confirm via a link we email you (double opt-in). Creating a play account or using magic-link sign-in does not subscribe you. Soft 13+: for readers 13+. Do not subscribe if under 13.
  • What we send. Edition emails and studio news you asked for. We do not sell your email. We do not use Newsletter email for unrelated third-party ads.
  • What we keep. Email, confirm time, how you opted in, and unsub/bounce/complaint signals.
  • How you leave. One-click unsub in every email, or blockbros1808@gmail.com; honor ASAP (target <24h).

Related: play.blockbros808.com/privacy/ · /terms/ · /news/

7. Children's privacy

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you're under 13, please don't create a Block Bros account, sign in, connect a wallet, use BlockChat, use Ask AI, put a game on Square or a shelf, load a game to your phone, or subscribe to Blockbros News. Parents: if a child under 13 signed up or gave an email, contact blockbros1808@gmail.com — we will delete that account/email.

Some games (like International Robot League) use kid-safe design choices for players 13 and up — for example, quick-chat with preset phrases only, not free typing. That does not mean the Services are directed at children under 13.

If you're a parent and think your child under 13 gave us personal information, email blockbros1808@gmail.com and we'll delete it.

8. Your rights and choices

Wherever you live, you can ask us to:

  • See the personal information we have about you
  • Fix anything that's wrong
  • Delete your information (except blockchain records, which we can't change, and anything we must keep by law)
  • Get a copy of your information in a usable format
  • Object to or limit certain uses
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on it (like connecting a wallet). Just disconnect.

Email blockbros1808@gmail.com with your request. We may need to confirm it's really you, for example by asking for your username or guest ID. We'll reply within a reasonable time and within any deadline the law sets. We won't treat you differently for using your rights.

You can also:

  • Browse and play as a guest without signing in
  • Clear cookies and local storage in your browser (this may reset saved progress and the same-day hub visitor flag)
  • Choose not to connect a wallet

If you're in the EU, UK, or a similar region: we process information to provide the game you asked for (contract), for our legitimate interests (keeping games fair, safe, and working), with your consent (optional features like wallet connect), or to follow the law. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

If you're in California: you have the rights above under the CCPA/CPRA, including the rights to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We don't sell or share personal information, so there's nothing to opt out of. We don't use sensitive personal information to infer things about you. You can use an authorized agent to make requests.

9. Where information is processed

We're based in the United States, and our platforms may store data in the U.S. or other countries. By using our Services, you understand your information may be processed outside your home country, where privacy laws may be different.

10. How long we keep information

We keep information only as long as we need it:

  • Leaderboard and gameplay data: while the game or leaderboard is live, or until you ask us to delete it
  • Guest data saved on your device: until you clear it
  • Emails to us: as long as needed to help you, then deleted within a reasonable time
  • Block Bros accounts: kept while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account (email blockbros1808@gmail.com or use in-app delete when offered), we delete or anonymize account email, settings, and private history within a reasonable time. Public Square listings, scores, or nicknames already shown may remain unless you ask us to remove them where we can. Review and magic-link emails stop when the account is deleted.
  • Blockbros News email (optional): email, confirm time, how you opted in, and unsub/bounce/complaint signals (see Section 6). One-click unsub in every email, or email blockbros1808@gmail.com.
  • Hub safety / ops signals (staff only): about 30 days, then deleted or rolled into anonymous totals
  • Hub visit count totals (anonymous): day and path totals kept for a limited period (up to about a year of daily totals), then dropped
  • Hub game like totals (anonymous): per-game counts kept while the game is listed (or rolled into anonymous totals)
  • Hub like lock tokens (anonymous): salted one-way tokens kept so a device cannot re-like the same game after the same-day undo window; not tied to a name or account; dropped if we remove the lock system or delist the game
  • Blockchain data: permanent on the blockchain and outside our control

11. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, like relying on established platforms and limiting who can access data. No online service is 100% secure, so please don't put personal info in names, and never share your wallet seed phrase.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our games change. We'll update the "Last updated" date at the top. If we make big changes, we'll post a notice on our site or in the game.
